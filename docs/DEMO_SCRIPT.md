# Hackathon pitch: 5 minutes

**One line:** "Like Bolt or inDrive, but for licensed professionals: verified businesses post a need, qualified experts respond, and every step is protected."

## The story to tell
1. **Problem (30s).** A mason has a tender but needs a licensed architect today. A supplier needs a pharmacist's consultation by tomorrow. Today they phone around, with no proof of who they are dealing with and no protection on the money.
2. **Trust first (45s).** Both sides are verified: CIPC-registered business with a signed Service Delivery Standards on one side; licence-checked professional, candidate or graduate on the other. Show the gate refusing an unverified business.
3. **Say it in your own words (30s).** Type a rambling request into the AI intake. It picks the profession and whether it is a consultation or a licensing cover.
4. **Bolt-style matching (60s).** Only that niche is alerted. Offers and counter-offers arrive, the expert sees their take-home before bidding, the entrepreneur picks.
5. **Money that is safe and fast (45s).** Pay by card or instant EFT into a hold, expert confirms delivery, funds release, 85/15 split, payout to their verified account. Show a dispute holding the money.
6. **The licensing moat (60s).** Both parties swear affidavits, each reads the other's redacted copy, both confirm. Show changing a term voiding the old affidavits.
7. **Safe meetups (30s).** Risk score, trusted contact, escalation ladder, SOS.
8. **Close (30s).** POPIA built in (export, erase, minimal data), 16 end-to-end tests, and a clear path to FNB business banking: linked business accounts and payouts.

## Likely judge questions
- *How do you make money?* Tiered fee, 15% down to 7%, R50 minimum, only on completed deals.
- *Why would people not go off-platform?* Contact details hidden until a pick, escrow protection, ratings, and the licensing paperwork only exists here.
- *Is this legal?* Verification, sworn affidavits and anti-fronting terms are designed in; funds-holding and the affidavit templates are on the lawyer-review list.
- *What is real vs mocked?* Be honest: payments, SMS and register checks are mocked behind interfaces; the business rules and tests are real.
